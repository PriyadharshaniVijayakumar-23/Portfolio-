01 / OVERVIEW

About the project

PetCare is a mobile app concept for pet owners who want a simple way to organize important pet-care tasks. The design brings reminders, pet details, and health records together in a clear, approachable interface.

02 / THE CHALLENGE

Keeping track of pet care can get difficult.

Pet owners may need to remember vaccination dates, medicine schedules, feeding routines, and vet visits. When these details are scattered across notes or memory, it can be easy to miss an important task.

Design goal

Create a friendly, straightforward mobile experience that makes key pet information and upcoming care tasks easier to find.

03 / DESIGN PROCESS

From wireframes to final UI

I first explored the structure using low-fidelity layouts, then applied the PetCare visual style to the final screens.

Wireframes

Early layouts focused on screen hierarchy and where each feature should live.

04 / FINAL DESIGN

A warm, simple visual style

The final screens use soft pink surfaces and a deeper rose accent to make the app feel welcoming while keeping important information easy to scan.
